Before we start

  • What we keep: a numeric face template (not a photo), your wallet address and the name you pick.
  • Photos: the camera pictures are used once to make the template and are never saved.
  • Why: the bin recognises you so your reward is paid to your wallet.
  • Your control: you can delete your face data at any time from this page.
  • Demo: payouts are Solana devnet test tokens with no real value.

The problem

One greasy container can ruin a whole batch of recycling.

Sorting rules are local and confusing, smart bins classify material instead of what your city accepts, and there is little reason for anyone to get it right. CashCan fixes all three at the bin.

Your city's rules, not a material label

Every decision is looked up against a local rule table, so the bin says where an item really goes in Metro Vancouver, and why.

Catches contamination

A top camera and a front camera must agree. Food-soiled or greasy items are flagged and earn a reduced reward instead of slipping through.

A reason to get it right

Reward-eligible items pay out instantly to your Solana wallet. The hauler that buys the sorted material funds the rewards.
